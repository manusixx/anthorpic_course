# Nutrition Plan for Muscle Gain

## Daily Caloric Intake
- **Target**: ~2,800-3,200 calories/day (surplus for muscle growth)
- **Protein**: 170-185g daily (2g per kg bodyweight)
- **Carbs**: 350-400g daily (post-workout priority)
- **Fats**: 80-90g daily (healthy sources)

## Protein Sources ✓
- **Lean meats**: chicken breast, turkey, lean beef
- **Fish**: salmon, tuna, cod, mackerel (avoid shellfish)
- **Eggs**: whole eggs and egg whites
- **Dairy**: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, milk
- **Plant-based**: lentils, chickpeas, tofu, tempeh
- **Protein powder**: whey, plant-based (avoid peanut-based)

## Carbs (Pre/Post-Workout)
- Oats, rice, pasta, quinoa
- Sweet potatoes, regular potatoes
- Whole grain bread
- Fruits: bananas, berries, apples

## Healthy Fats
- Olive oil, avocado oil
- Nuts: almonds, walnuts, cashews (NO peanuts)
- Seeds: chia, flax, sunflower
- Fatty fish: salmon, mackerel

## Sample Daily Meal Structure
1. **Breakfast**: Oats + eggs + banana
2. **Snack**: Greek yogurt + berries
3. **Lunch**: Chicken + rice + vegetables
4. **Pre-workout**: Banana + almonds
5. **Post-workout**: Protein shake + carbs
6. **Dinner**: Salmon + sweet potato + broccoli
